# Seven-minute presentation storyline

1. Problem: reproduce 1998–2008 behavior, then make transparent conditional 2008–2028 scenarios.
2. Data: 100 static reservoir realizations, 70/15/15 split, observed field history; no future labels.
3. Data-quality finding: compare cumulative-derived interval rates, not unaligned boundary rates.
4. Benchmark: best supplied match and what it proves.
5. Proxy validation: selected model, validation-first selection, isolated test result.
6. Non-uniqueness: accepted parameter combinations and why surrogate matches do not prove true geology.
7. Forecast: ten traceable scenarios, water burden, explicit assumptions.
8. Dashboard: field outlook, match evidence, uncertainty, water decisions; close with validation and simulator-needed next step.
